# Venue Booking SOP — Aurora Atlas Events

This standard operating procedure defines how event staff of Aurora Atlas Events select, book and manage event venues. It applies to every client event from 50 to 5,000 attendees and to internal events with more than 100 attendees. The VenueOps system is the single source of truth: a venue hold that is not recorded in VenueOps does not exist for planning purposes, and payments made outside VenueOps are a reportable process violation.

## 1. Venue tiers

Venues are classified into three tiers in VenueOps. Platinum venues are flagship properties: convention centres and five-star hotels with in-house production teams. Gold venues are premium independent hotels and dedicated event spaces with an approved AV partner. Silver venues are community halls, boutique spaces and university auditoria. The tier is assigned by the Venue Operations team during onboarding and reviewed annually. Clients may request any tier, but the tier drives the lead times and deposit rules in this SOP.

## 2. Lead times

Platinum venues must be booked at least 45 days before the event date. Gold venues must be booked at least 30 days before the event date. Silver venues must be booked at least 14 days before the event date. A booking inside these lead times requires the regional operations manager's approval recorded in VenueOps before a hold is requested. Holds are placed in VenueOps with a note on the requesting client, and competing holds on the same date are resolved by the operations manager in order of contract signature date.

## 3. Site visits

A physical site visit is mandatory for every event expected to exceed 500 attendees, and recommended for all others. The visit covers access routes, loading docks, power distribution, rigging points and accessible entrances, and is documented on the site-visit checklist in VenueOps. A virtual walk-through is accepted only for silver venues under 200 attendees. Two staff members attend every platinum site visit, and the checklist must be uploaded within 3 business days of the visit.

## 4. Deposits and payments

A deposit of 25 percent of the venue fee is payable within 10 days of confirmation, calculated from the day VenueOps marks the booking as confirmed. The balance is invoiced 30 days before the event and must be settled 7 days before the event. Late deposit payment releases the hold automatically back to the open market, and VenueOps notifies the booking owner on the day the deposit becomes due. For silver venues the deposit is refunded in full if the client cancels before the payment due date.

## 5. Cancellation penalties

When a client cancels a confirmed venue booking, the following penalty scale applies to the venue fee. Cancellation 90 or more days before the event: 10 percent of the venue fee. Cancellation 60 to 89 days before the event: 25 percent of the venue fee. Cancellation 30 to 59 days before the event: 50 percent of the venue fee. Cancellation less than 30 days before the event: 100 percent of the venue fee. Penalties are passed through to the client per the engagement letter, and any waiver needs the operations director's signature. Venue credits issued by the property instead of a refund are recorded in VenueOps with their expiry date.

## 6. Capacity and safety

The venue's certified capacity must exceed the expected attendance by at least 10 percent, including staff and contractors. Events with more than 1,000 attendees require public liability insurance with a minimum cover of INR 50,000,000 per occurrence, arranged through the insurance desk before the deposit is paid. A floor plan marked with exits, assembly points and barricades is uploaded to VenueOps at least 15 days before the event. The emergency exit walk-through with the venue manager happens 5 business days before the event and is signed off in VenueOps by the event lead.

## 7. Production and AV

The AV checklist, including power, screens, sound, lighting and streaming requirements, is uploaded to VenueOps 15 days before the event. Platinum venues provide in-house production and one joint technical rehearsal is scheduled 3 days before the event; gold and silver venues need an external AV partner from the approved list. Rigging above audience seating needs the venue's structural sign-off. Any change to the AV checklist inside 72 hours before the event is treated as a change request and priced by the venue at its rack rate.

## 8. Invoicing and closure

Final reconciliation is completed in VenueOps within 20 days after the event. The event lead uploads the actual attendance count, damage report if any, and all venue invoices. Venue Operations reviews each reconciliation monthly and escalates disputes older than 30 days to the finance desk. Venues with three or more disputes in a rolling year are suspended from the approved list pending a review.

## 9. Backup planning

For every platinum booking, the owner records one backup venue of the same tier in VenueOps with a hold note, so a late venue-side failure can be absorbed. Backup holds are cancelled as soon as the event passes the 30-day mark. Event leads running multi-venue programmes must keep a single backup venue list to avoid duplicate holds.
