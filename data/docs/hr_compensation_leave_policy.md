# HR Compensation and Leave Policy — Aurora Atlas Events

This policy sets out salary structure, bonuses, and every category of leave for employees of Aurora Atlas Events Private Limited. It is visible to the HR team for payroll processing, offer preparation and audits. Employees receive their personal terms in the offer letter; this master document is the reference HR uses to answer policy questions and to resolve exceptions. It is synthetic internal documentation for a portfolio project and contains no real person's data.

## 1. Salary structure

Salaries are organised in five bands, A through E, each with a defined base range and a band premium of 10 percent per band above the previous one. Band A covers entry roles, band B covers specialists, band C covers senior specialists and team leads, band D covers managers, and band E covers senior managers and above. Offers are positioned between the 25th and 75th percentile of the band range; anything outside the range needs the HR director's signature. Basic pay is 50 percent of the cost to company, and the remainder is house rent allowance, transport allowance and flexible benefits.

## 2. Bonus and appraisal

The appraisal cycle runs each April, with results effective in July. Annual bonus is 8 to 12 percent of base pay depending on the performance rating: a rating of 4 or 5 earns 12 percent, a rating of 3 earns 10 percent, and a rating of 2 earns 8 percent. A rating below 2 means no bonus and a performance improvement plan. Bonus is paid with the July payroll to employees who have served at least 6 months of the cycle year and are not under notice on payment day.

## 3. Annual leave

Employees earn 24 paid days of annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 6 days may be carried forward into the next calendar year; anything beyond 6 days lapses at the end of March. Leave is applied through the HR portal with the manager's approval, and more than 5 consecutive working days needs the manager's and HR's approval. Leave encashment at exit is paid at 60 percent of the basic daily rate for each unused day, capped at 15 days.

## 4. Sick leave

Sick leave of 12 days per year is granted without a medical certificate for absences of up to 2 consecutive days. A certificate from a registered practitioner is required beyond 2 days, and hospitalisation needs the discharge summary. Unused sick leave does not carry forward and is not encashed. Long-term illness beyond 30 consecutive days moves to short-term disability with 70 percent of basic pay, reviewed by HR each 30 days.

## 5. Parental leave

Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave as per statute, taken in one block starting before or after delivery, with a further 12 months of unpaid return right on request. Paternity leave is 10 working days within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption, taken in up to 3 blocks. Adoption leave mirrors maternity leave for the primary caregiving parent for children under 12 months. A creche reimbursement of INR 6,000 per month applies for two years after return.

## 6. Notice and separation

The notice period is 30 days for bands A to C and 60 days for bands D and E. Notice may be bought out at one month of basic pay per waived month with the reporting manager's consent. Full and final settlement is completed within 45 days of the last working day. Relocation allowance of up to INR 150,000 applies to moves requested by the company between cities, with receipts, and is repaid on a sliding scale if the employee leaves within 12 months of the move.

## 7. Long-term incentives

Band D and above receive long-term incentive grants with a 4-year vesting schedule, 25 percent per year, subject to continued service on each vesting date. The grant value is proposed by the department head, approved by the HR director and recorded in the compensation register. Grants do not transfer between group companies. A leaver forfeits the unvested portion on the last working day.

## 8. Probation and records

New joiners serve a 6-month probation, extendable once by 3 months with a written reason. Confirmation requires the manager's completion checklist and HR's document audit. Salary revisions happen once a year in the July cycle, except promotion increases, which are effective from the promotion month. Payroll records are retained for seven years. Exceptions to any clause in this policy need the HR director's written approval filed against this document's number in the HR portal.
