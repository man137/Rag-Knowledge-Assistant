# Visa and Travel Advisory FAQ — Aurora Atlas Events

This FAQ explains how Aurora Atlas Events supports international travellers attending or producing events: visa timelines, passport requirements, insurance, vaccinations and what happens when a visa is refused. All consular support runs through the GlobalAssist portal. Travel booked for a trip also follows the Travel and Expense Policy, but this FAQ answers the visa questions specifically.

## 1. Where to start

Every international trip starts with a risk check on the GlobalAssist portal. GlobalAssist publishes advisory levels for every destination: green for normal travel, amber for heightened caution, and red for avoid travel. Amber destinations need a manager's written approval before any booking, and red destinations are not travelled to under any circumstances until the advisory is downgraded. The advisory level on the booking date governs the whole trip, so travellers should re-check the portal if plans change.

## 2. Passport requirements

Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the planned return date and contain at least 2 blank visa pages. Renewal applications in India typically take 3 to 6 weeks, so check the passport first. Travellers holding non-Indian passports should confirm their own country's renewal timelines with GlobalAssist, which maintains a per-nationality checklist. Name mismatches between passport and ticket, for example after marriage, must be corrected before the visa appointment.

## 3. Business visa timelines

For most business visa destinations, standard processing takes 10 to 15 business days from the day the embassy accepts a complete application. Expedited processing takes 3 to 5 business days and costs an additional INR 6,500, reimbursable as part of the travel claim with the embassy receipt. Schengen visas typically take 15 to 20 business days because applications pass through the visa centre before the consulate. Start the application at least 45 days before departure for standard processing and 20 days for expedited processing. Peak seasons, such as embassy holidays and the months of April and May, add roughly 30 percent to these timelines.

## 4. Application process

Embassy appointments are booked through the GlobalAssist portal, which holds a standing appointment allocation with the major visa centres. The traveller provides the invitation letter, event registration confirmation, bank statements and employment letter; GlobalAssist fills the application form, books the appointment and uploads the file. Biometrics are captured at the visa centre in person. GlobalAssist sends a checklist reminder 10 business days before the appointment, and incomplete files are returned once with a 24-hour window to fix them.

## 5. Insurance and health

Travel medical insurance is mandatory for every international trip and must carry at least USD 100,000 of medical coverage including repatriation. The corporate policy arranged through the insurance desk meets this threshold and is included in the trip cost; travellers buying their own cover must send the policy schedule to GlobalAssist before departure. A yellow fever vaccination certificate is required for travel to countries on the government advisory list, and the certificate is checked at the airport by the airline. Travellers carrying prescription medicines must carry the doctor's prescription in English.

## 6. Refusals and problems

If a visa is refused, contact the GlobalAssist desk within 2 business days of receiving the decision. GlobalAssist reviews the refusal reason, advises whether a reapplication or an appeal is realistic, and drafts the cover letter for the next attempt. A second refusal for the same traveller and destination triggers a review call with the line manager before any new application is paid for. Trip costs already committed are handled under the force majeure and cancellation rules of the booked suppliers, not refunded automatically by the company.

## 7. Work permits and event staff

Event crew travelling to set up booths or production equipment often need a different visa category than business visitors. GlobalAssist checks the category per destination before booking, because working on a visitor visa can lead to entry refusal and a multi-year ban. Crew travelling with equipment also carry the carnet or temporary export documents arranged by the logistics desk; missing carnet papers are the most common cause of customs delays on arrival.

## 8. Costs and reimbursement

Embassy fees, visa centre service charges and expedited fees are reimbursed through the AtlasPay expense portal within the Travel and Expense Policy timelines: submit the claim within 15 calendar days of the decision or refusal letter. Reimbursement covers one standard application and one expedited upgrade per trip. Applications for personal travel routed through corporate accounts are charged back to the employee's payroll.
