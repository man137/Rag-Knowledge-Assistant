# Event Registration FAQ — Aurora Atlas Events

This FAQ answers the most common questions about registering for events produced by Aurora Atlas Events, including our standard, premium and VIP tiers, group bookings, cancellations, transfers and refunds. All registrations happen through the AtlasEvents Hub. Prices are per attendee unless stated otherwise, and every tier includes access to the keynote hall and the exhibition floor.

## 1. Registration tiers and pricing

The standard tier covers keynote access and the exhibition floor. The premium tier adds the hands-on workshops and includes lunch on both days. The VIP tier adds front-row keynote seating, the speakers' meet-and-greet and a dedicated concierge desk. An early bird discount of 20 percent applies to registrations made 30 or more days before the event start date and cannot be combined with other discounts. Prices include applicable taxes, and a GST invoice is issued automatically after payment.

## 2. How to register and pay

Create an account in the AtlasEvents Hub, choose the event, pick a tier and pay by card, UPI or net banking. Corporate registrations can be invoiced against a purchase order with 14 days payment terms. Registration is confirmed only after payment or an accepted purchase order. Tickets are personal and carry the attendee name; badges are printed at the desk with the name from the Hub profile. Registrations close 48 hours before the event or earlier if the venue reaches capacity.

## 3. Cancellation and refund rules

The refund rules below apply to every tier unless the event page states otherwise. If you cancel 30 or more days before the event start date, you receive a full refund. If you cancel 15 to 29 days before the event start date, you receive a 50 percent refund. If you cancel less than 15 days before the event start date, you receive no refund. A no-show receives no refund. Refunds are always processed to the original payment method within 14 business days of the cancellation being confirmed by our desk. Card processing fees charged by the payment gateway are not returned.

## 4. Transfers and name changes

A registration can be transferred to another person free of charge, once per registration, up to 7 days before the event start date; after that a transfer is treated as a cancellation under section 3. Transfers are requested from the registration page in the Hub and re-issued as a new badge at the desk. Name corrections, such as a misspelled surname, are free at any time before the event. Premium workshops cannot be transferred separately from the main ticket.

## 5. Group bookings

A group booking covers 10 or more attendees registered and paid in one transaction. Group bookings cancelled 30 or more days before the event receive a 75 percent refund of the total paid. Later cancellations follow the standard rules in section 3 per seat. Group organisers can add seats at the original price up to 14 days before the event if capacity allows, and receive one complimentary standard ticket for every 20 paid seats.

## 6. Force majeure and event changes

If an event is cancelled by the organiser, attendees receive a full refund or a credit note valid 12 months, at the attendee's choice. If an event is postponed, tickets transfer automatically to the new date, and attendees who cannot attend the new date receive a refund under the force majeure terms. Weather, government restrictions, venue failure and speaker cancellations beyond our control are handled under this section. Travel booked for a postponed event follows the travel supplier's own cancellation terms and is not covered by this FAQ.

## 7. Accessibility, dietary and special requests

Accessibility support, including wheelchair seating, sign interpretation and quiet rooms, is requested through the Hub at least 10 days before the event so the venue team can prepare. Dietary requirements for included lunches are collected during registration and passed to the caterer; last-minute dietary changes cannot be guaranteed. Press and photography accreditation is requested through the communications desk at least 5 business days before the event.

## 8. Contact

Registration questions go to the registration desk through the AtlasEvents Hub help widget. Billing questions go to the finance desk with the invoice number. On-site issues go to the concierge desk for VIP guests or the main helpdesk for all other attendees. Our support team answers registration questions within 2 business days and event-week questions within 4 business hours.
