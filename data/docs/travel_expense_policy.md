# Travel and Expense Policy — Aurora Atlas Events

This policy applies to every employee and contractor of Aurora Atlas Events Private Limited who books travel for client events, venue inspections, supplier meetings or approved internal gatherings. It covers booking rules, spending caps and the reimbursement process. It does not cover client-billed travel, which is governed by the project budget approved in the engagement letter. Where a client contract and this policy conflict, the stricter of the two applies and the difference must be approved in writing before booking.

## 1. Booking principles

All travel must be booked through the Aurora Travel Desk portal at least 21 days before departure, except when a client confirms an event inside that window. Travellers must select the lowest logical fare: the cheapest option that matches the approved schedule and arrival time. Split ticketing to defeat fare rules is prohibited. Bookings made outside the portal without a written exception are reimbursed at the lowest logical fare only, and repeated violations are escalated to the traveller's manager.

## 2. Flights

Economy class is the default for every flight of 6 hours or shorter, door to door. Business class may be booked only when both conditions hold: the flight is longer than 6 hours and a manager has approved the exception in the Aurora Travel Desk before purchase. First class is never reimbursed. Change fees are reimbursed up to INR 3,500 per change when the change is caused by a client request or a documented schedule conflict; cancellation fees caused by personal reasons are the traveller's own cost. Seat selection fees are reimbursed for flights longer than 4 hours only.

## 3. Per diem and meals

For domestic trips the meal allowance is INR 2,500 per day. For international trips the meal allowance is USD 75 per day, converted at the reference rate published on the expense date. An additional incidental allowance of INR 500 per day covers tips and small local expenses; receipts are not required for per diem amounts. Per diem is paid at full rate for travel days and at 50 percent for a day with a company-provided lunch or dinner, such as a gala evening at the event.

## 4. Hotels

The hotel cap is INR 9,000 per night in metro cities, defined as Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and Pune, and INR 6,500 per night in all other cities. For international travel the cap is USD 180 per night. Staying inside the cap is the traveller's responsibility; the portal flags properties above the limit. If the only available property near an event venue exceeds the cap by more than 25 percent, a written exception must be obtained before check-in. Laundry is reimbursed only for trips longer than 5 nights, up to INR 800 per trip.

## 5. Local transport

Rideshare, taxis and rental cars with drivers are capped at INR 1,500 per day. A receipt is required for any single journey above INR 500. Car rentals driven by the traveller require manager approval and full insurance; fuel and tolls are reimbursed with receipts. Parking at the home-base office is not reimbursed. Airport transfers booked through the portal are exempt from the daily cap because they are billed to the event cost centre directly.

## 6. Cash advances and corporate cards

The corporate card is the default payment method for flights and hotels. A cash advance may be requested through AtlasPay for out-of-pocket destinations, up to 80 percent of the estimated trip cost. Advances must be settled within 10 business days of returning: either as expense claims that absorb the advance or by returning the balance. Unsettled advances older than 30 days are recovered from the next payroll cycle and noted in the traveller's file.

## 7. Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted in the AtlasPay portal within 15 calendar days of the trip end date. Managers must approve or return a claim within 5 business days of submission. A receipt is mandatory for every individual expense over INR 1,000; per diem amounts do not need receipts. Currency conversion uses the central reference rate on the expense date, and conversion charges are reimbursed up to 2 percent of the converted amount. Late submissions beyond 45 days need a written reason and are paid at the manager's discretion.

## 8. Lost receipts and exceptions

A signed lost-receipt declaration is accepted for amounts under INR 5,000, at most twice per traveller per year. Larger amounts without receipts are not reimbursed. Any expense above a cap in this policy needs a written exception approved by the Vice President of Finance before it is incurred. The following are never reimbursed: alcohol, minibar charges, traffic fines, hotel gym or spa fees, personal entertainment and equipment for personal use. Suspected duplicate or inflated claims are referred to Internal Audit, and confirmed violations lead to disciplinary action under the employee code of conduct.

## 9. Records and review

Approved claims are stored in AtlasPay for seven years for audit. Finance Operations samples 10 percent of claims each quarter for receipt verification. Teams planning a series of trips for one event may request a pooled travel budget; pooled budgets carry the same caps and require a monthly reconciliation review. Questions about this policy go to the Finance Operations helpdesk in the AtlasEvents Hub; disputes about an approved claim escalate to the Head of Finance, whose decision is final for amounts below INR 200,000.
